Definition 5.1.

A game matrix for a game with two players, Rose and Colin, is a matrix with each row representing a choice that Rose can make and each column representing a choice that Colin can make. The \((i,j)\) entry of the matrix is the pair \((a,b)\text{,}\) called the \textit{payoff vector}, where \(a\) is the payout for Rose and \(b\) is the payout for Colin if Rose chooses \(i\) and Colin chooses \(j\text{.}\)
