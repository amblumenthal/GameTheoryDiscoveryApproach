Definition 6.25.

[Equated Results] Let \(A\) be a zero sum matrix game. A strategy \(\textbf{p}\) for Rose equates Colin's Results if all entries of \(\textbf{p}A\) are equal. Likewise, a strategy \(\textbf{q}\) for Colin \textbf{equates Rose's Results} if all entries of \(A\textbf{q}\) are equal.
