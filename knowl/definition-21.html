Definition 6.28.

[Von Neumann Solution] Let \(A\) be a zero sum game with mixed strategies for Rose and Colin \(\textbf{p}\) and \(\textbf{q}\) respectively which both have a guarantee of \(v\text{.}\) The triple, \(v, \textbf{p},\) and \(\textbf{q}\) are called a \textit{von Neumann Solution}.
