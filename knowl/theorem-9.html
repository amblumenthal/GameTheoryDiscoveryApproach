Theorem 6.23.

\(\textbf{p}\)\(\textbf{q}\)\(A\text{.}\)
  1. If \(\textbf{p}\) has a guarantee of \(r\text{,}\) then \(\textbf{p}A\textbf{q} \geq r\text{.}\)
  2. If \(\textbf{q}\) has a guarantee of \(c\text{,}\) then \(\textbf{p}A\textbf{q} \leq c\text{.}\)
  3. If \(\textbf{p}\) and \(\textbf{q}\) have guarantees of \(r\) and \(c\text{,}\) then \(r \leq c\text{.}\)
