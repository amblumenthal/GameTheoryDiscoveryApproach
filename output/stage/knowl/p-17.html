In situations like this, we could say that Pick Up Stones 2 with \(n\) and \(m\) stones is the same as Pick Up Stones with \(n\) stones \(+\) Pick Up Stones with \(m\) stones. The two games being added together are often called components of the game. In sums of games, on a player's turn they choose one component to play in and make one move in that game, then their turn ends. (Just like with Pick Up Stones 2)
