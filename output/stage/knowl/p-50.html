We now turn our attention to mixed strategies, but need to build tools to deal with them first. Recall that a mixed strategy can be written as follows: \([(r_1,r_2,\dots,r_m),(c_1,c_2,\dots,c_m)]\) where \(r_i\) is the probability that Rose selects row \(i\) and \(c_j\) is the probability that Colin selects column \(j\text{.}\)
