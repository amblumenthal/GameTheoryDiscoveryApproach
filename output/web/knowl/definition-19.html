Definition 6.20.

Let \(\textbf{p}\) be a strategy for Rose. The guarantee of \(\textbf{p}\) in a game \(A\) is the \textit{minimum} entry of the vector \(\textbf{p}A\) (since Colin will choose the lowest value of these options). Conversely, the guarantee of a strategy \(\textbf{q}\) for Colin is the \textit{maximum} entry of the vector \(A\textbf{q}\) (since Rose will choose the largest value of these options).
