Question 6.31.

Imagine that Rose and Colin are playing tennis at a high level. Analytics have been done to determine the odds of Rose winning a given point based on the following scenarios. Rose can either hit to Colin's forehand (F) or backhand side (B), and since the ball is moving so quickly, Colin must guess which side, forehand (F) or backhand (B) that Rose will hit to. If Colin guesses wrong, he will not get his racket as solidly on the ball. The data analysts have determined that if both play F, Rose has an \(20\%\) chance of winning the point, if the both play B Rose has a \(50\%\) chance of winning the point. If Rose plays F and Colin guesses B, she has a \(80\%\) chance of winning, and if Rose plays B and Colin guesses F she has a \(90\%\) chance of winning.
