Dating Dilemma: Suppose Rose and Colin have started dating and they are texting to decide what to do this evening. Colin would like to go to the basketball game \(B\) and Rose would like to watch a film \(F\text{.}\) Colin is irresponsible and didn't charge his phone, so it dies before a decision is made, but they both know the two options. They both value time apart as \(0\) utility, time together as \(1\) utility at the event they didn't suggest, and \(2\) utility for being together at the event they suggested.
